# Akiteru Maeno

**Research Imaging | Micro-CT | 3D Visualization | Scientific Image Analysis | Scientific 3D Printing**

I am a research technical specialist working at the intersection of biological imaging, comparative morphology, and 3D visualization.

My work focuses on the practical workflow from specimen preparation and Micro-CT imaging to 3D reconstruction, image analysis, scientific visualization, and physical 3D modeling.

## Expertise

- Micro-CT imaging of biological specimens
- 3D reconstruction and volume visualization
- Scientific image analysis
- Segmentation and morphological analysis
- Biological and comparative morphology
- Scientific 3D printing
- Research visualization for publications and presentations
- Research workflow improvement and automation

## Tools & Technologies

- Micro-CT
- OsiriX MD
- Imaris
- Adobe Premiere Pro
- Python
- Git / GitHub
- 3D printing

## Current Interests

I am particularly interested in integrating imaging, 3D visualization, digital modeling, and research workflow automation to make biological structures easier to analyze, understand, and communicate.

I also develop small tools and workflows for practical research use.
